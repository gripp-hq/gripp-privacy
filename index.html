Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 October 2026

The short version: Gripp sends nothing anywhere. No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reports, no cloud copy. Everything it knows - your transactions, your receipts, your screen time - lives on this phone and is deleted with the app.

ON THIS PHONE ONLY

  • Bank and e-wallet transactions, read from notifications.
  • Scanned receipts and their photos.
  • Utility bills. Name cards. Screen time per app. Your settings.
  • Records you scan, such as medical certificates and reports.
  • Uninstalling deletes all of it, unless you made a backup file with Back up everything in Settings.

WHAT GRIPP ASKS FOR

  • Notifications - this is how transactions are captured.
  • Usage access - which apps held your attention, and for how long.
  • Installed apps - so you can pick which ones carry your money.
  • Camera and photos - only when you scan something.
  • Microphone - only while you speak a question to Ask.
  • It never reads SMS, and never will.

WHAT LEAVES THE PHONE

  • Nothing, unless you send it.
  • Export and Back up everything hand a file to Android. You choose where it goes.
  • Tapping an e-invoice link on your own receipt opens that shop's page.
  • Call, WhatsApp or Email hands the number to that app.
  • Each of those is you sending something, in the moment you asked.

THE ONE EXCEPTION

  • Receipts are read on this phone.
  • Paste an Anthropic key in Settings and the receipt TEXT is sent to Anthropic to be tidied up.
  • Your photos are never sent, with or without a key.
  • The scan screen says so each time. No key ships in the app.
  • With a key, a question in Ask also sends your receipts' text.
  • It sends the payments your bank announced to shops, too.
  • And app names and minutes, your bills, and name cards.
  • From a name card: the name, company and title only.
  • Never a phone number, an email, an address, or the name of a person you paid or who paid you.
  • The Ask bubble names what is sent while it is sent.
  • Your records are sent only if you switch on "Let Ask read my records" in Settings. It is off unless you switch it on.
  • With a key, "Explain this record" sends that one record's printed text when you tap it.
  • It puts the printed words into plain language and gives no advice. The photo stays on your phone.

VOICE, IF YOU SWITCH IT ON

  • The microphone in Ask listens on this phone. Your voice stays here.
  • Some languages have no on-phone listener. Gripp then asks, once, whether to let Google hear you instead.
  • If you say yes, your voice goes to Google's servers and the words come back.
  • Gripp keeps none of it, and the box says "Listening through Google" every time it happens.
  • It is off unless you switch it on, and Settings switches it off again.

NEVER

  • No data is sold, to anyone, for any purpose.
  • No advertising. No tracking. No analytics. No crash reporting.
  • No account, no sign-in, no server, no cloud copy.
  • No location. Gripp never asks for it and cannot read it.

CHILDREN

  • Gripp is a tool for adults. It is not aimed at children.

CHANGES

  • If any of this changes, this page will say so before it does.
